    HOW TO PLAY

    Controls

    • Move arrow keys, or A and D
    • Jump Up, W or Space. Tap it just before you land and it still counts; let go early for a shorter hop
    • Wall slide push into a wall while falling, then jump to kick off it
    • Drop through a trap door S or Down, on the courses that hide them
    • Use an item X or Shift
    • Turn an item Q and R, before you confirm it
    • Reset yourself R, or the button in Settings. It counts as a death
    • Chat / or T
    • Phones get buttons on screen

    A match

    • The host makes a room and sets the rules. Everyone else joins from the room list, by six-letter code, or by a player's ID
    • Join a match already running and you watch that round, then join in on the next one
    • Everyone votes on a course from three choices. Red H courses are harder; C are made by players; EE are event courses
    • Each round you take one item and place it, then everyone runs at once with a single life
    • 20 seconds to take an item, then 20 to place it. Slower than that and you lose the turn
    • Traps stay for five rounds, then the room votes on a new course
    • Now and then a round has a twist: low gravity, an ice age, haste, an earthquake, or rarely a blackout
    • First to the points target wins. Tie at the top and they settle it in a Final Battle

    Traps and blocks

    • Spikes kill on touch. They need a block behind them
    • Long Spikes three tiles of them
    • Crumbler looks solid, gives way just after you land
    • Gum slows you to a crawl and cuts your jump. Sticks to any side of a block
    • Bumper flings you away from its middle
    • Spring launches you high, then rests two seconds
    • Ice slippery. Goes on top of a block
    • Decoy looks like spikes to everyone else, does nothing
    • Plank a solid bridge, three tiles wide
    • Big Block a solid 2 × 2 block
    • Mover a block that slides back and forth and carries you
    • Mirror a solid glassy block. Thrown balls bounce off it
    • Conveyor Belt 2 to 5 tiles long, carries you sideways. Turn it to flip the direction
    • Fan blows a draft four tiles up, lifting runners and thrown balls
    • Generator powers a Fan, Spring or Belt it touches. Chain up to three. Runs three seconds, rests two
    • Eraser rubs out someone else's trap instead of placing one. One per course
    • Pencil draw up to three short lines of blocks. They last the round

    Tap the course to set your item down, drag to move it, then press E, tap ✓, or tap the same spot again. Nothing goes within two tiles of the flag.

    Pickups

    Placed in open air, collected by the first runner to touch them. Three each per round; two of the same means two goes with it.

    • Teleport Ball hold X to aim, let go to throw. You appear where it lands
    • Baseball Bat knock a thrown ball away. Whoever threw it still lands where you sent it
    • Shield soaks two hits
    • Revive Heart one more life
    • Rocket Boots one extra jump in mid-air, per landing
    • Feather you float
    • Speed Shoes 35% faster

    Final Battle weapons

    • Rocket Boots one extra jump in mid-air
    • Dash a burst forward
    • Shield survive one spike
    • Freeze Ray freeze the others for a moment, once
    • Trap Bomb drop spikes under your feet, once
    • Feather floaty gravity for the run

    Points

      No points if everyone finishes, or everyone dies. Your own trap never pays you.

      Chat commands

      • /mute NAME hide that player's chat. /unmute NAME undoes it. Anyone can
      • /kick NAME throw a player out
      • /ban NAME [5|30|120|1440|forever] keep them out, in minutes
      • /changemap NAME pick the next course
      • /item NAME [PLAYER] in a Test Match, take an item or hand it over
      • /maxplayers N room size, in the lobby
      • /lobby end the match. /start begin one. /help this list

      All but /mute are the host's. A name after a command always means somebody else, never you.

      Lobby settings

      • Time limit how long a run may last
      • Pick time and Place time the two clocks in a round
      • Points to win and Round cap when the match ends
      • Point values what a win, a trap kill, a trailblazer and an autonomous finish are worth
      • Max players and Listed room size, and whether strangers can find it

      Level Editor

      • Paint blocks, spikes and movers, set a start and a flag, then test it
      • Saved courses live in your browser
      • A host can add their saved courses to a room for everyone to vote on
