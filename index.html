    HOW TO PLAY

    Controls

    Move with the arrow keys or A / D. Jump with Up, W, or Space. Tap jump just before you land and it still counts. Let go early for a shorter hop. Phones get buttons on screen.

    A match

    The host creates a room and sets the rules in the lobby. Everyone joins with the six-letter code and picks a color (you can change it until the start). When the host presses Start, everyone gets ten seconds to vote on the first course.

    Each round, every player places one trap by tapping the course. Once everyone has placed, everyone runs at once with a single life. Reach the flag before the clock runs out. Traps stay for three rounds, then the room votes on the next course.

    The match ends when someone reaches the points target, or when the round cap is hit and the top score wins. If two or more players qualify at once, they fight a Final Battle: one more run, no traps placed, everyone else watching.

    Traps

    Each round you are dealt one trap at random (key 1 selects it). Spikes kill on touch. A Crumbler looks like a platform but gives way a moment after you land on it. Glue slows you to a crawl and you can't jump out of it. A Bumper bounces you away from its center, so the angle you hit it at decides where you fly. The Eraser (key 5) removes one of someone else's traps instead of placing yours; you get one per course.

    Final Battle weapons

    Before a Final Battle each fighter is offered three weapons and picks one. Rocket Boots: one extra jump in mid-air. Dash: X or Shift for a burst forward. Shield: survive one spike hit. Freeze Ray: X or Shift freezes the other fighters for a moment, once. Trap Bomb: X or Shift drops a spike trap under your feet, once. Feather: floaty low gravity for the whole run.

    Points

      No points if everyone finishes, or everyone dies. Your own trap never pays you.
