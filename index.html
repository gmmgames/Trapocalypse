    HOW TO PLAY

    Controls

    Move with the arrow keys or A / D. Jump with Up, W, or Space. Tap jump just before you land and it still counts. Let go early for a shorter hop. Push into a wall while falling to slide down it slowly, and jump while sliding to kick off the wall. Phones get buttons on screen.

    A match

    The host creates a room and sets the rules in the lobby. Everyone else presses Join Room and picks a room from the list, or types a private room's six-letter code at the top. You can also invite a friend by the six-character player ID shown at the bottom of their screen; they get a Join button. Pick a color (you can change it until the start). When the host presses Start, everyone gets ten seconds to vote on the first course.

    Each round, every player picks an item and places it on the course. Once everyone has placed, everyone runs at once with a single life. Reach the flag before the clock runs out. Traps stay for five rounds, then the room votes on the next course.

    The match ends when someone reaches the points target, or when the round cap is hit and the top score wins. If two or more players qualify at once, they fight a Final Battle: one more run, no traps placed, everyone else watching.

    Traps

    Each round the room is offered a random handful of items, and everyone picks one. Once someone takes an item, nobody else can take it while others are still free. Tap the course to set your item down, move it if you like, then press E, tap the ✓, or tap the same spot again to confirm. Spikes kill on touch. A Crumbler looks like a platform but gives way a moment after you land on it. Gum slows you to a crawl and cuts your jump to a stubby hop; it has to be stuck to a block on some side (floor, ceiling or wall) and shows which. A Mover is a block that slides back and forth: stand on it and it carries you. A Plank is a solid block three tiles wide that stays for the whole course: a bridge, a step, or a roof over someone's spikes. A Bumper bounces you away from its center, so the angle you hit it at decides where you fly. A Spring launches whoever lands on it high into the air, which helps or hurts depending on what's up there. Ice is slippery: you keep sliding after you stop pushing. A Decoy looks exactly like spikes to everyone but you, and does nothing. The Eraser removes one of someone else's traps instead of placing yours; you get one per course. The Pencil is rare: picking it uses your build turn, and during the run you hold the mouse (or a finger) on the course to sketch a short line of blocks to stand on. Sketches last until the round ends, and you get three per pencil. The Teleport Ball is rarer still: you place it as a glowing orb, and during the run the first runner to touch it picks it up. Press X / Shift (or USE) to throw it in the direction you face, and you appear wherever it lands, so aim carefully.

    Final Battle weapons

    Before a Final Battle each fighter is offered three weapons and picks one. Rocket Boots: one extra jump in mid-air. Dash: X or Shift for a burst forward. Shield: survive one spike hit. Freeze Ray: X or Shift freezes the other fighters for a moment, once. Trap Bomb: X or Shift drops a spike trap under your feet, once. Feather: floaty low gravity for the whole run.

    Points

      No points if everyone finishes, or everyone dies. Your own trap never pays you.
